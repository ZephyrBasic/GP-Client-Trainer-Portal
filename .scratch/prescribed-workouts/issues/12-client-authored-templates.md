# 12: Client-authored Workout Templates

**What to build:** A Client saves a routine of their own — one their Trainer didn't prescribe — and starts it exactly
like an assigned one. A Workout Template is one kind of thing whoever wrote it; only the author may
edit it, and a Client's own Template is simply not prescribed.

**Blocked by:** 04 (A Trainer can author a Workout Template), 07 (A Client performs a live Session)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Client can create and name their own Template from catalog Exercises
- [ ] It appears alongside prescribed workouts and starts the same way
- [ ] A Client's own Template is not shown as prescribed
- [ ] A Client cannot edit a Template their Trainer authored
- [ ] A Trainer cannot edit a Template their Client authored
- [ ] Typecheck clean
