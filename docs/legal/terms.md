## 1. What GreenPulse is

A workout tracker. A trainer writes workout plans, assigns them to their clients with individual
target loads, and reviews the sessions those clients record. A client performs sessions against
those plans, or records sessions of their own.

**GreenPulse is a record-keeping tool. It is not a coach, and it is not a medical service.** It does
not assess whether an exercise is safe for you, whether a load is appropriate, or whether you should
be training at all. See section 6.
